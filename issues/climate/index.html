Climate challenges are urgent, interconnected, and demand local action supported by global cooperation.

(01.1)

Climate Overview

A variety of environmental pressures—pollution, water scarcity, deforestation, and biodiversity loss—are accelerating the climate crisis and affecting millions of communities.

(01.2)

Pollution

Air and water pollution degrade ecosystems and create major public health risks, especially in underserved regions.

(01.3)

Water Scarcity

Water stress is rising as demand grows and climate impacts intensify, threatening agriculture, health, and livelihoods.

(01.4)

Deforestation & Soil Health

Forest loss and soil degradation reduce resilience and accelerate carbon emissions, making restoration essential.
